## Current position

Since January 2024 I have been a senior teaching and research fellow (Oberassistent) in German linguistics at the chair of Noah Bubenhofer, Department of German Studies, University of Zurich. I teach independently, work on my habilitation project *Postdigital writing cultures in the context of LLMs* and coordinate the project *AI in teaching: promising practices in the humanities and social sciences*. Since 2026 I have been a member of the executive committee of the International Max Planck Research School for Multimodal Digital Humanities.
