I want students to be able to analyse linguistic phenomena independently, empirically and critically, a competence that gains relevance in times of social polarisation and generative AI. Part of this is not treating methods as neutral: students should understand how procedures structure data and shape findings. I developed my teaching concept over about ten years in Zurich, Heidelberg, Basel and Geneva and reflected on it systematically in the Certificate of Advanced Studies in Higher Education at the University of Zurich.

Three principles guide my courses:

1. **Constructive alignment.** I align learning objectives, activities and assessments and communicate them transparently. Writing conventions and assessment logics are treated as learnable practices that are made explicit and supported step by step.
2. **Research-based learning.** Students work close to the sources, sometimes with unexplored material, and go through a full research cycle from question to communication of results, for instance as a poster presented to a school class.
3. **Transferable skills.** Critical thinking, peer feedback and audience-oriented communication are part of every course. With generative AI, a reflective approach to one's own writing process becomes central.

## At a glance

- Independent teaching at BA and MA level at the universities of Zurich, Basel, Geneva and Heidelberg: 9 introductory courses, 21 thematic seminars, 4 lecture courses, 9 summer school courses.
- Areas: discourse, media and cultural linguistics, semantics, pragmatics, corpus linguistics and corpus pragmatics, interactional linguistics, modern language history, academic writing.
- PhD workshops at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University Skopje.
- Design and coordination of the summer school *Zürcher Korpuslinguistik und Korpuspragmatik* (ZuKoKo) with Noah Bubenhofer, since 2021.
- First supervisor of eleven BA and MA theses, second reader of five, coaching of four doctoral projects.
- More than twelve teacher-training courses and four digital learning platforms.
