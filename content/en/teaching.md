## Teaching philosophy

In my courses, students work with data and sources and have the opportunity to get hands-on experience of the different phases of linguistic research. I do not treat methods as neutral: whoever understands a procedure knows which findings are due to the object and which to the tool. This applies to digital tools in general and corpus analysis tools in particular as much as to the use of generative AI. I am also particularly concerned with introducing students to a thought style in Ludwik Fleck's sense: they should understand language as social practice and be able to analyse linguistic phenomena independently, on an empirical basis and critically. I align learning objectives, learning activities and assessments with one another and make them transparent from the start. I also treat writing and communication conventions as well as examination logics as learnable practices.

## Other teaching formats

With Noah Bubenhofer I have designed and coordinated the summer school *Zürcher Korpuslinguistik und Korpuspragmatik* (ZuKoKo) since 2021. I also give regular training courses for teachers, mainly on the Matura thesis and on writing with AI, and have co-developed several digital learning environments, among them *KoDuP*, an introduction to corpus analysis with Python for students of German.
