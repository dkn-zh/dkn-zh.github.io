In my courses, students work with data and sources and have the opportunity to get hands-on experience of the different phases of linguistic research. Besides introductory courses in the diachronic and synchronic linguistics of German, these are above all thematic courses in corpus linguistics and corpus pragmatics, discourse, media and cultural linguistics, semantics and pragmatics, and academic writing. I do not treat methods as neutral: whoever understands a procedure knows which findings are due to the object and which to the tool. This applies to corpus tools as much as to generative AI.

With Noah Bubenhofer I have designed and coordinated the summer school *Zürcher Korpuslinguistik und Korpuspragmatik* (ZuKoKo) since 2021. I also give regular training courses for teachers, mainly on the Matura thesis and on writing with AI, and have co-developed several digital learning environments, among them *KoDuP*, an introduction to corpus analysis with Python for students of German.
